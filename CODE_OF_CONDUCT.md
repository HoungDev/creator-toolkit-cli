# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward others.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Trolling or insulting comments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove contributions that violate these guidelines.
